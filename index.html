About this page

  • Each line is a single political TikTok account, traced across monthly snapshots from July 2025 onward. To keep the field continuous and meaningful, the page shows only consistently active accounts — those with data in every month that drew at least 500,000 views in every month. Lines are colored by partisan lean: Left-Leaning, Neutral, and Right-Leaning.
  • Posts is the number of videos published in the month. Views and Engagements are the totals that account's content received that month (engagements = likes, comments, shares, and saves).
  • Charts use a linear vertical scale, so equal vertical distances represent equal differences in value — the gap from 100M to 1B is ten times the gap from 1M to 10M. As a result the very largest accounts sit far above the pack. Hover a line to trace it (the others fade back), click it to lock it open so you can read each month without slipping onto a neighbour, and click again or press Esc to release it.
  • Partisan lean is a subjective editorial judgment, not a scientific measure — reasonable people will disagree. Spot one you'd label differently? Let us know.
  • Use the legend to show or hide a lean group, and the search box to find a specific account. Account names link to their TikTok profiles.
